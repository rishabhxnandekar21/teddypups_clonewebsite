## 13. Implementation priorities

Do not begin detailed visual tuning from this provisional document alone. Once inspection is complete, prioritize:

1. Verified page routes and complete section order.
2. Accurate visible copy, imagery, and primary navigation/CTA destinations.
3. Main container geometry, header, hero, and major section layouts.
4. Typography, spacing, colours, image crops, and component styling.
5. Mobile/tablet behaviour and interactive states.
6. Keyboard accessibility, loading/error behaviour, and final regression checks.
